## Research Question: Are there "nightlife" type areas where crime concentrates late at night, and how do they differ from areas with daytime-dominant crime

## Boston Crime Report

**Provider:** Boston Police Department, issued by the City of Boston Department of Innovation and Technology.

**Link:** https://data.boston.gov/dataset/crime-incident-reports-august-2015-to-date-source-new-system

## Relevant Variables

| Variable | Description | Data type | DC Correlation | Role/Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| OCCURRED_ON_DATE | Date and time the incident could have taken place | START_DATE | Used to time the features so that we can cluster them into: late-night,weekend-night, and daytime |
| UCR_PART | Category and seriousness of the offense | OFFENSE | Classification inputs are matched to DC's groups, UCR_PART ensures accurate comparison of offenses  |
| DISTRICT, REPORTING_AREA | Geography | DISTRICT, PSA | Locations of where crimes took place. |
| STREET | Street name | BLOCK | |
| |---|---|---|
